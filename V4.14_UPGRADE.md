# Zento v4.14 — Story / Support / Calls UX Upgrade

- Story rail is forced to render above the chat and is refreshed after opening chats and resizing.
- Story reply uses a custom Zento modal instead of the browser prompt.
- Story owner statistics pause the story while open and resume video from the previous position.
- Story view/reaction totals are hidden from other viewers; only the owner sees totals and detailed stats.
- Support center has three sections: live support conversation, feedback/suggestions, and contact, with topic dropdown and an in-panel send button.
- Profile modal back button is wired correctly; profile avatar can open in the image viewer.
- Dark mode now uses high-contrast text and controls for better readability.
- Audio/video call controls received a more polished visual treatment.
- Group conference calling was added with peer-to-peer WebRTC signaling and up to 8 remote participants per room.
- Group conference includes mute, camera toggle, screen sharing, and a clear hang-up control.
- Direct calls keep audio/video controls; group chats expose the conference action.
- App cache version was bumped to prevent stale JavaScript/service-worker copies from masking fixes.
