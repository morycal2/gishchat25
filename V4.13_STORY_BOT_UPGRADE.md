# Zento v4.13 — Advanced Stories & BotFather

- Instagram-style story bar is rendered in the chat header area and sidebar.
- Story composer: image/video preview, filters, text overlay, drawing, square crop, rotation, tagging, captions, audience controls.
- Story playback respects 10s images and up to 45s video; optional trim start/end is honored by the viewer.
- Story replies use Zento's own modal UI; no browser prompt/Google UI.
- Story visibility: everyone, nobody, everyone except selected users, selected users only.
- Private group/channel join flow supports membership requests.
- @username mentions can open users or public groups/channels; join/request buttons are shown.
- Native Zento BotFather account is seeded and supports /start, /help, /newbot and /mybots in a direct chat.
